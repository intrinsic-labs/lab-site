## What it is

A paid, offline-first utility for a musician putting together an album or EP. You import the audio you already have — voice memos, demos, mixes — through the Files picker, organise it into albums and tracks, attach the metadata a release needs (writers, BPM, key, genre, lyrics, liner notes, artwork), and play it back through a custom player. It is not a recorder; it never touches the microphone.

## How it is built

SwiftUI with the Observation framework, SwiftData for the library, AVFoundation for playback behind a small provider protocol, iOS 17 and up, iPhone only. Straight paid-up-front: no in-app purchases, no accounts, and the privacy label says truthfully that the developer collects no data.

## Status

Version 1.0.2 has been live on the App Store since October 2024. The source is not public — this is the one project in this section sold rather than cloned. The repository's tip carries an unreleased experiment (sign-in plus streaming) that must not ship as it stands, so the next update is a cleanup: strip that branch, restore a dropped fix for audio files surviving app updates, add the privacy manifest, and then an on-device pass before it goes out.
