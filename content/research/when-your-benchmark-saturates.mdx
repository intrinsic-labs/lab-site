A pipeline mines steering decisions from AI agent transcripts. Stage 2 grades each candidate moment — is this a genuine steer, and how strong? Fourteen held-out items and six models answered this:

| engine | exact | HIT / not-HIT | s/item |
|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.6-27B | 8/14 | **10/14** | 31 |
| Qwen3.8-27B | 8/14 | 9/14 | 89 |
| Claude Sonnet | 8/14 | 9/14 | 9 |
| Claude Opus | 7/14 | 8/14 | 14 |
| Claude Fable | 7/14 | 8/14 | 13 |

Everything competent clusters at 8–10 of 14. At n=14 every point is ±7%. This sheet is a terribleness filter; it cannot rank survivors.

Shared misses aren't all model error. A moment models read as a durable delegation policy was labeled MISS because the person was trying out a tool feature. That's context no prompt carries. The sheet is partly saturated.

## What replaced ranking

When a sheet can't rank, stop asking it to: **filter → disagreement-mine → label the disagreements.**

Run two survivors over 300 stage-1 candidates, stratified round-robin by project, with exemplars removed first. Identical prompt across engines. Both engines completed all 300 with zero failures.

```
unanimous on HIT / not-HIT : 80%  (60 split)
unanimous on 3-way grade   : 73%  (81 split)
"mine it" rate:   Qwen3.6  46%     Sonnet  34%
```

The confusion matrix:

| | strong | weak | none |
|---|---|---|---|
| **strong** | 90 | **45** | 3 |
| **weak** | 9 | 85 | 7 |
| **none** | 3 | 14 | 44 |

Of 60 binary splits, 48 are "Qwen mines it, Sonnet doesn't." 45 of those sit in one cell: Qwen says *strong*, Sonnet says *weak*. Only 6 items in 300 are a strong↔none flip.

**The engines are not reading differently. They are cutting the same ordering at different places.** Where they disagree is exactly where a human label buys the most information. The question isn't "which model understands the corpus"—it's *where does the HIT/WEAK line belong*.

## Where this goes next

The 60 binary splits are the labelling queue. What it buys: the labels place the line, and whichever engine sits on the right side runs the full 3,800-candidate pass. The question stops being which model is better and becomes where the boundary is.
