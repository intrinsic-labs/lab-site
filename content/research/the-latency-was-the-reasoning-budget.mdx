This field note comes out of the local-model grading round for [Tycho](/products/tycho), the program that builds [The Ghost](/products/tycho#the-ghost).

Qwen3.8-27B ran at default "high" reasoning and measured **38–256 seconds per item**, clustering near two minutes. Its predecessor did the same items in about 31 seconds. The natural read: the newer model was too slow.

A colleague said it "smells like we haven't gotten the model working properly yet." Fair point. Same slice, same prompt, reasoning off.

**With reasoning disabled, Qwen3.8 was the fastest local engine in the round** and finished all 300 items in 70 minutes. The latency had been the thinking budget.

Three specifics from the retest:

**Check what landed in the response, not just the flag.** The `reasoning_effort` parameter is accepted whether or not the runtime honours it. What proves it took effect is `completion_tokens_details.reasoning_tokens` and `message.reasoning_content` on the way back. All 300 reasoning-off rows came back with zero reasoning content; every earlier row had it. Without that audit trail, "we ran it with reasoning off" is an assertion.

**"MLX is faster on Apple Silicon" is a rule of thumb.** MLX quantizations landed hours after GGUF. Head-to-head on the same slice, the MLX 4-bit build ran ~40 s/item against GGUF's ~29. Worse, MLX accepted both knobs and silently ignored them; only llama.cpp/GGUF honoured them. GGUF also ships speculative decoding, which accounts for most of its win.

**A 4× headline is relative to its own default.** Dropping to `reasoning_effort: low` moved one item from 152 s to 34 s. Over a wider run, "low" still measured 38–256 s. The 4× was real relative to the high tier. It did not make the model fast. Off did.

## Where this goes next

Accuracy with reasoning off was measured on the same 300-item slice but hasn't been compared here. That's the open question: does disabling reasoning cost little in agreement, or a lot? The answer decides whether the full pass runs locally at these speeds on hardware we own.
