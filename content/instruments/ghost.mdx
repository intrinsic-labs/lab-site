Ghost is a pipeline for building a high-fidelity model of one person's judgment — not their prose style, their *decisions* — from the record they have already left: several years of conversations with AI coding agents and chat assistants, across four different tools.

## The stages

**Adapters** parse each source's export format into one normalized event stream. This layer is pure code — regex, role checks, field extraction — and nothing in it knows who the person is. A scrub pass strips secrets and personal identifiers before anything downstream sees a byte.

**Stage 1** finds candidate *steering moments*: places where the human interrupted, negated, redirected, or — the one that took a calibration pass to discover — agreed at length after loading a lot of context. The detectors are structural (turn position, interrupt events, message length relative to the person's own distribution) plus a small vocabulary of steering phrases. About half of them are generic; the other half were tuned to one person and would need re-tuning for anyone else.

**Stage 2** grades each candidate with a model: is this a real steer, and what kind? The taxonomy was derived from twenty hand-labelled items, and the grading prompt carries a handful of verbatim exemplars that never leave the private corpus.

**Holdout**, enforced in code: a fixed slice of sessions the compile step can never read, so evaluation has something honest to measure against.

**Eval**: a judge model compares the persona's answer to what the person actually said, with the judging itself audited by a second pass — because the first finding of the project was that the judge was wrong about two-thirds of the time.

## What is published, and what never is

The methodology — the information-asymmetry construct, benchmark saturation and disagreement mining, the meta-judge gate, revealed-versus-stated preferences — is generalizable and contains no personal data. That is what appears in the research index. The corpus itself is one person's private record; it has no remote, and it will not be released in any form.
